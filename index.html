About

Strategy, executed at the track.

I am a motorsports marketing and public relations professional whose work spans venue leadership, team and driver representation, series activation, and brand development. My experience covers NASCAR, ARCA, NHRA, rally, and short-track racing. As Director of Marketing & PR at Newport Speedway and founder of Octane Influence, I have led a venue revival, built sponsorship pipelines, and developed brand audiences from the ground up.

My academic training in social work, with a degree from King University, informs how I approach fans. It gives me a grounded understanding of community behavior and audience psychology, and of what earns lasting loyalty. That perspective is uncommon in motorsports marketing, and it shapes every program I build.

I do not build campaigns. I build belonging.

Newport Speedway

From junkyard to sold out

Challenge
Newport Speedway, a 3/10-mile oval with 26-degree banking, had been dormant since its final race in November 2017. When new ownership acquired the property in 2024, crews had to remove hundreds of tons of scrap vehicles and metal from what had become a junkyard before racing could resume. The task was to restore East Tennessee’s confidence in the venue.
What I did
Directed the relaunch marketing and communications strategy and built a sponsorship program spanning multiple divisions.
Result
Sold-out events at a venue that had sat dormant for seven years.
