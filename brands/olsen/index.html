The making of an Olsen

Slow hands

A Porsche can be fast on the day it leaves Stuttgart. Making one right again takes a small room of people who would rather spend another hour than ship the car early.

Paint, trim, engine and suspension are handled under one roof in Downers Grove. One owner, one build sheet, and a bench where the same hands see the car from teardown to first start.
